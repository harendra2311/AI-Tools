Our story

One reliable partner for the things you reorder every week.

Abasyn International was founded on a simple idea: running a hotel is hard enough without chasing a dozen different suppliers for the things you reorder every week. We bring it all together — towels, linens, amenities, slippers, laundry and housekeeping essentials — under one reliable partner, at prices that respect your bottom line.

Headquartered in Toronto, we work directly with manufacturers and global sources to import quality hospitality goods and deliver them to hotels across North America. Hotels are where we start, but our ambition is bigger: to grow into a full-scale import and export company connecting trusted producers with the businesses that depend on them.
