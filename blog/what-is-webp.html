What Is WebP and Why Should You Use It?

2026-05-016 min read

WebP is an image format designed for efficient lossy and lossless compression on the web.

Why it helps

Many photos become meaningfully smaller than JPG at similar visual quality, which improves load time.

When to convert

  • Marketing sites and blogs that already serve modern browsers
  • Product galleries with dozens of photos per page

Use JPG to WebP or PNG to WebP to prepare assets.
